# Bo Peng: academic homepage

Source of my personal website, **https://bop2000.github.io/BoPeng-HomePage/**

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Data Science at City University of Hong Kong, working on AI for science. I build data-efficient optimization algorithms and AI agents, and apply them to architected materials (metamaterials), alloy design, quantum systems and the optimization of complex systems.

## About this repository

A static site (HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript) published with GitHub Pages. There is no build step.

- Fonts: Instrument Sans and BP Serif (a renamed subset of IBM Plex Serif), both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. License texts are in `assets/fonts/`.
- Icons: Font Awesome Free, CC BY 4.0.
- Paper figures come from the publications they illustrate. The research map at the top of the page is an inline SVG drawn for this site.
